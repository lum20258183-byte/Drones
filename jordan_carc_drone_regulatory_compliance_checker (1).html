Applicable Legal Provisions
Article 3 • Special Committee 11 Members

Inter-Agency Drone Committee

Chaired by CARC Director of Aviation Security & Facilitation, comprising representatives from the Ministry of Interior, JAF (Military Security, Intelligence, Drone Directorate, Military Judiciary, Royal Jordanian Air Force), GID, PSD, Jordan Customs, and CARC Security Standards/Oversight.

Meets at least monthly to review technical specs, operational purposes, transport/storage rules, and recommend licensing to the CARC Board President.
Enforcement & Confiscation Rules
  • Art. 6/A: Unlicensed border imports or unauthorized individual/commercial flights inside Jordan are subject to immediate confiscation by Customs or Security Agencies.
  • Art. 7: Licenses may be suspended or revoked for exceeding altitude/path/time limits or 6 consecutive months of inactivity without excuse.
  • Art. 8: Commercial registry or license changes require prior CARC President approval.
